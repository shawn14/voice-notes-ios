Get the thoughts out of your head—and find what matters later.

Press Record and think out loud. EEON turns your voice into readable notes, extracts action items and decisions, and lets you ask questions across your saved notes.

A BRAIN DUMP YOU CAN USE

A walk, a busy afternoon, or the moment after a conversation: speak naturally instead of organizing everything first. Keep your original transcript alongside a clearer version of your note.

KEEP THE FOLLOW-UPS

See the tasks pulled from your notes and mark them complete. Enable Apple Reminders to keep those actions in an EEON list alongside the reminders you already use.

REMEMBER WHAT YOU DECIDED

Ask questions such as:
- What did I decide about the launch?
- What follow-ups did I mention for Lena?
- What ideas came up in my notes this week?

EEON uses your saved notes to help you revisit the context, instead of making you listen through every recording.

CAPTURE IN YOUR OWN WAY

Record a voice note, type a note, or import an existing recording. Search your library and save useful notes as favorites. Calendar connections are optional and can add meeting context.

YOUR NOTES, YOUR CONTROL

Notes are stored on your device and can sync through your private iCloud account. Audio and text may be sent to AI services for transcription and note enhancement. Optional AI agent access shares an encrypted copy of your notes and knowledge articles after you authorize the connection. See the privacy policy for details.

FREE TO START

Try recording and transcription for free. EEON Pro unlocks unlimited AI notes, formats, and full memory features. Available as an auto-renewing monthly or annual subscription; current prices and included features are shown before purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.
